
# MedDefense Clinical Downtime Procedures

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared by:** Nishat Sultana Supty  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** Draft - Pending Clinical Review  
**Audience:** Nurses, physicians, laboratory staff and unit clerks

These quick-reference guides explain how to continue patient care when Epic EHR or the Laboratory Information System (LIS) is unavailable.

## Quick Reference 1: Epic EHR Downtime Procedures

**For nurses, physicians and unit clerks**

### 1. Report the outage and confirm downtime

If Epic is unavailable, notify the charge nurse immediately.

- The IT Incident Commander (ext. 7200) confirms the outage.
- The Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) confirms Epic's status.
- **The Administrator on Call (ext. 7000) authorizes hospital-wide downtime.**
- The Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100) coordinates nursing units.

**Do not assume Epic is safe to use until restoration is confirmed.**

Follow the charge nurse's instructions and begin approved paper documentation when downtime is declared.

### 2. Collect paper forms

Use the forms stored in your clinical area.

**ICU**

- Red "Downtime Forms" binder: ICU charge nurse desk.
- Paper medication administration records (MAR): locked lower drawer under the charge nurse workstation.
- Allergy verification cards: wall pocket beside the medication room door.
- Emergency contact list: laminated card attached to the binder.

**Emergency Department**

- Downtime binder: charge station, cabinet 3, top shelf.
- Paper triage forms: triage desk, left drawer.
- Paper MAR forms: Pyxis room wall file.

**Medical-Surgical Unit**

- Downtime binder: central nursing station, blue binder rack.
- Physician order sheets: unit clerk desk, right cabinet.
- Paper MAR forms: medication room, shelf above the barcode scanner docking station.

### 3. Continue patient care safely

1. **Confirm the patient's identity before treatment or medication administration.**
2. Record assessments, vital signs and care on the Clinical Assessment Sheet.
3. Physicians must document new orders on paper Physician Order Sheets.
4. Document verbal orders using the Verbal Order Read-Back Form.
5. **Verify allergies before administering medication.**
6. Record each medication administered on the paper MAR, including the required administration details.
7. Keep patient records organized and accessible to authorized clinical staff.

If essential information cannot be confirmed, contact the responsible clinician before proceeding.

### 4. Escalate urgent concerns

For patient-care concerns, follow this escalation path:

**Primary nurse → Charge nurse → Nursing Supervisor → Attending physician → Administrator on Call.**

For medication concerns, contact:

**Nurse → Charge nurse → Pharmacist on duty (ext. 7400) → Attending physician.**

Escalate immediately when missing information or delayed care creates a patient-safety concern.

### 5. Reconcile records after Epic returns

1. Wait for confirmation that Epic is available.
2. Collect all paper assessments, medication records and physician orders.
3. Use the Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet from the unit clerk desk.
4. Check that each paper record is complete and belongs to the correct patient.
5. Enter or reconcile downtime information using the hospital's approved process.
6. **Check carefully for duplicate medication administration or repeated orders.**
7. Have the charge nurse and responsible clinicians review unresolved differences.

**Do not discard paper records until reconciliation and approved record handling are complete.**

## Quick Reference 2: Laboratory Information System Downtime Procedures

**For laboratory technicians, nurses and ordering physicians**

### 1. Report the outage and confirm downtime

If laboratory orders or results cannot be accessed, notify the laboratory supervisor and responsible clinical unit.

- The IT Incident Commander (ext. 7200) confirms the outage.
- The Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) confirms LIS status.
- **The Administrator on Call (ext. 7000) authorizes hospital-wide downtime.**
- The Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100) coordinates affected nursing units.

Follow the laboratory's approved manual workflow while LIS is unavailable.

**Urgent laboratory testing and critical-result communication must continue.**

### 2. Locate paper forms

**Laboratory**

- Yellow "LIS Downtime" binder: accessioning desk.
- STAT Lab Requisition: accessioning desk, tray 1.
- Routine Lab Requisition: accessioning desk, tray 2.
- Critical Value Call Log: chemistry bench clipboard.
- Manual Specimen Tracking Log: accessioning desk.
- Specimen Rejection Log: accessioning desk, tray 3.

**Emergency Department**

- Lab requisition forms: specimen collection cart, second drawer.

Nurses and physicians should use the appropriate paper requisition when electronic ordering is unavailable.

### 3. Submit and identify specimens

1. The ordering clinician completes a paper requisition.
2. Clearly indicate whether testing is STAT or routine.
3. **Confirm the patient's identity before specimen collection.**
4. Include all required specimen information:
   - Patient's full name.
   - Medical record number (MRN).
   - Date of birth.
   - Collection date and time.
   - Collector's initials.
   - Requested test.
   - Ordering provider.
5. Send the specimen and requisition through the approved laboratory collection process.
6. Laboratory accessioning staff record receipt in the Manual Specimen Tracking Log.

**Do not process an unidentified or mismatched specimen.** Record the issue in the Specimen Rejection Log and follow the laboratory's rejection procedure.

### 4. Escalate STAT order problems

For urgent laboratory order issues, follow this escalation path:

**Ordering clinician → Lab accessioning (ext. 7500) → Chemistry Lead (ext. 7510) → Laboratory Medical Director (ext. 7520).**

Inform the ordering clinician when delays may affect patient treatment.

### 5. Communicate critical results

**Critical results must be communicated directly, not left waiting for electronic delivery.**

1. The laboratory calls the ordering unit.
2. Communicate the patient identification, test and critical result.
3. **Require the receiver to repeat the result back.**
4. Record the receiver's name, call time and read-back in the Critical Value Call Log.
5. If there is no answer within **5 minutes**, escalate to the charge nurse.
6. If there is still no answer within **10 minutes**, escalate to the Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100).

Continue the approved escalation process until the result reaches the responsible clinical team.

### 6. Reconcile records after LIS returns

1. Wait for confirmation that LIS is available.
2. Gather paper requisitions, tracking logs and critical-result call records.
3. Match each paper order and result to the correct patient.
4. Reconcile outstanding specimens and unreported results.
5. **Check for duplicate orders, missing results and incorrectly matched specimens.**
6. Confirm that critical-result communications are documented.
7. Ask laboratory and clinical leads to resolve any discrepancies.

**Preserve all downtime records according to the hospital's approved documentation procedures.**
