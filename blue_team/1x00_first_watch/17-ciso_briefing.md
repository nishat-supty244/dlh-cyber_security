# Board Briefing: MedDefense Security Status

MedDefense is currently facing a high-risk cybersecurity situation. Critical security weaknesses are putting patient information and healthcare operations at risk.

An unauthorized attacker is currently inside our systems, and this breach could escalate into a ransomware attack at any moment.

Our network architecture does not have enough separation between systems. If an attacker gains access to a single device, they could potentially take control of the entire network, including critical medical equipment such as life-support infusion pumps.

Recent attacks on nearby hospitals followed a similar pattern, causing millions of dollars in damage, long operational shutdowns, and risks to patient safety.

## Immediate Priorities

- **Stop the active breach on the billing server immediately**  
  Cost: $3,500 (Week 1)

- **Create network divisions to separate clinical and financial data environments**  
  Cost: $8,000 (Month 1)

- **Install centralized security monitoring tools to detect and stop threats early**  
  Cost: $10,000 (Month 2)

These improvements are part of the requested **$120,000 security budget**. This investment is designed to prevent potential losses exceeding **$5 million**, including fines, recovery costs, and lost revenue reported by similar regional healthcare organizations.

This funding is not a standard IT expense. It is a critical investment to protect patient care, meet legal requirements, and safeguard MedDefense’s reputation.

Without these resources, MedDefense remains vulnerable to severe financial losses, complete operational disruption, and potential leadership consequences.
