# Executive Board Pitch: MedDefense Cybersecurity Strategy

## Current State

MedDefense currently operates an open, unsegmented **flat network** with unmanaged remote access points, exposing critical electronic health records (EHRs) and clinical systems to immediate compromise.

In short, our current security posture presents a **high-risk environment vulnerable to a catastrophic cybersecurity breach**.

---

## The Risk

Maintaining the current security posture leaves MedDefense exposed to a targeted ransomware attack that could exploit existing weaknesses, encrypt critical systems, and disrupt patient care operations.

A successful attack could result in:

- Clinical system outages
- Patient care disruption
- Regulatory penalties
- Data recovery expenses
- Operational downtime costs

**Projected Annual Financial Impact: $1,400,000**

**Risk Reference: Risk-001**

---

## The Plan

We recommend a comprehensive, risk-based cybersecurity defense strategy with a total investment of:

# **$120,000**

This strategy focuses on three foundational security controls:

### 1. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
**Objective:** Prevent unauthorized access caused by stolen or compromised credentials.

**Security Impact:**
- Blocks credential-based attacks
- Strengthens identity protection
- Reduces risk of account compromise

---

### 2. Network Segmentation (VLANs)
**Objective:** Prevent attackers from moving laterally across the environment after initial compromise.

**Security Impact:**
- Limits ransomware spread
- Isolates critical healthcare systems
- Improves access control and containment

---

### 3. Enterprise Endpoint Detection & Cloud Backups
**Objective:** Enable rapid threat detection, response, and recovery.

**Security Impact:**
- Detects malicious activity early
- Reduces incident response time
- Ensures operational recovery after attacks

---

## The Return on Investment

The proposed **$120,000 cybersecurity investment** eliminates an estimated:

# **$5,170,000 in Aggregate Annual Expected Losses**

This delivers an exceptional business return:

> **Every $1 invested avoids more than $43 in potential cybersecurity losses.**

---

## Executive Recommendation

Approve the **$120,000 cybersecurity modernization initiative** to reduce ransomware exposure, protect patient data, strengthen regulatory compliance, and ensure the continuity of critical healthcare services.
