
# MedDefense Health Systems
## Board Executive Brief — Unauthorized Access to Patient Records

**Incident:** IR-2026-0420-01  
**Prepared for:** Dr. Morales and the Board of Directors  
**Reporting date:** Monday, 21 April 2026 — 07:00 UTC (exercise scenario)  
**Classification:** Confidential  
**Status:** Preliminary investigation

### 1. What Happened

MedDefense identified unauthorized activity affecting hospital computers and a clinical file server.

The investigation records show that an account associated with a scheduling coordinator was used to access clinical documents outside the account holder's normal responsibilities.

Suspicious activity was also identified on four workstations and a scheduling server.

### 2. Impact on Patients

The available records report **1,847 access events involving patient-related PDF documents**, including laboratory results and clinical progress notes.

The recorded access occurred on 19 April 2026, between approximately 02:14 and 02:41 UTC.

The exact number of individual patients affected has not yet been established.

The investigation has not confirmed whether copies of these documents were transferred outside the organization.

### 3. Regulatory Position

**The preliminary assessment does not establish a low probability of patient-information compromise, so MedDefense should prepare for HIPAA breach notification, subject to final legal and privacy approval.**

### 4. Actions Taken and Outstanding

**Completed investigation work:**

- Identified the affected workstations and clinical file server.
- Identified the account associated with unauthorized access.
- Established the reported file-access window and categories of clinical information.
- Identified suspicious external communications.
- Prepared a targeted containment plan.

**Still outstanding:**

- Verify that affected systems have been isolated.
- Confirm that unauthorized account access has been stopped.
- Determine the exact number of affected patients.
- Establish whether patient information left the organization.
- Complete legal review and notification preparations.

No completed containment action has been independently verified from the evidence provided.

### 5. Leadership Priorities

Protect essential patient-care services while completing containment and preserving evidence.

Confirm the affected patient population and finalize the regulatory decision.

### 6. Next Update

**Next scheduled update:** Monday, 21 April 2026, 12:00 UTC (proposed).

**Update frequency:** Every six hours during the active response, with immediate escalation of any confirmed patient-safety impact or material change in the investigation.

**Responsible presenters:** Dr. Morales, with the incident response and legal teams.

**Board message:** Unauthorized access to sensitive clinical information has been identified. The exact patient impact remains under investigation, and the preliminary regulatory assessment supports preparing breach notifications.
